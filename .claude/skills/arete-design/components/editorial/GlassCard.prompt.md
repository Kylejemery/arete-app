One-line: the web app's glass surface — the only place transparency and blur are used in Arete.

```jsx
<GlassCard><Kicker style={{letterSpacing:'0.5px'}}>Marcus Aurelius</Kicker>
  <div style={{fontFamily:'var(--font-serif)',fontSize:17,lineHeight:1.5}}>Confine yourself to the present.</div>
</GlassCard>
<GlassCard tint="gold">How, when the calendar is next week?</GlassCard>
```

Glass belongs to `.web` only. Mobile and academy surfaces are opaque.
