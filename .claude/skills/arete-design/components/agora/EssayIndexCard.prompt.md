One-line: an essay on the web index — the Agora's counterpart to AcademyCard, used inside a `.academy` scope.

```jsx
<EssayIndexCard kicker="Essay" title="On being early to everything"
  author="Nadia Oyelaran" meta="Sep 12 · 9 min" tags={['Habit','Time']}
  excerpt="I arrive twenty minutes before I need to, and I have started to suspect it is not politeness." />
```

Keep the 2px radius. These are academic cards, not app cards.
