One-line: one comment in the flat list under an essay.

```jsx
<Comment author="Tomas Ruiz" time="2h">The arithmetic is the part I keep avoiding.</Comment>
<Comment counselor author="Seneca" time="1h">You asked whether earliness is a virtue. Ask instead what it buys you.</Comment>
```

Counselor comments use the italic-plus-accent-rule treatment the system reserves for anything a counselor says. Readers get no rule and no italic.
