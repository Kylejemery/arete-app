One-line: an essay in the Agora feed — title, author name, excerpt, topic kickers, comment count.

```jsx
<EssayRow title="On being early to everything" author="Nadia Oyelaran"
  meta="Sep 12 · 9 min" comments={14} tags={['Habit','Time']}
  excerpt="I arrive twenty minutes before I need to, and I have started to suspect it is not politeness."
  counselor="Seneca" onClick={open} />
```

Authors are names only. Never add an avatar or initial well to an essay author; the initial well belongs to counselors.
