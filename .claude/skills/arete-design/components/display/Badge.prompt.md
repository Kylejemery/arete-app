One-line: the tinted label that types a counselor (category), grades their bluntness (level), or marks paid/locked content.

```jsx
<Badge tone="stoics">stoics</Badge>
<Badge tone="direct">direct</Badge>
<Badge tone="gold">Best value</Badge>
<Badge tone="locked"><Icon name="lockClosed" size={12} /> Arete</Badge>
```

Category badges are 20% alpha of a hue with pastel text; levels are 15%. These hues are the only place non-gold color appears.
