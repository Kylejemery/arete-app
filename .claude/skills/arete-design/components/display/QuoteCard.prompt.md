One-line: a quotation from a counselor or text — 3px gold rule, italic warm text, gold attribution.

```jsx
<QuoteCard quote="Begin at once to live, and count each separate day as a separate life." attribution="Seneca" />
<QuoteCard icon={<Icon name="sunny" />} quote="At dawn, when you have trouble getting out of bed…" />
```

The `icon` variant is the morning affirmation: the whole quote turns gold.
