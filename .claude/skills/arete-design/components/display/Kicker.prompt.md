One-line: the tracked uppercase gold label that names a card, a counselor reply or a page section.

```jsx
<Kicker>Today's question</Kicker>
<Kicker dim>Prompt</Kicker>
<Kicker variant="eyebrow">Arete Premium</Kicker>
```

Kickers carry hierarchy in place of a display typeface. Counselor names above chat replies are kickers at 0.5px tracking.
